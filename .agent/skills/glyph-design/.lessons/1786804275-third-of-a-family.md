title: A third member of a silhouette family needs a solid mark to separate it at 18 px — a shape difference alone is not enough
date: 2026-08-15

The toolbox now has three brushes sharing one silhouette (round, flat,
watercolour). Two separate fine on shape alone — a tapering cone against a
squared blade reads at 18 px. The third does not: a _bellied_ head against a
straight-sided one is a difference the shipping size throws away, and the
contact sheet's 18 px column showed the round and the watercolour brush as the
same mark.

What separated them was ink, not outline: a filled dot in the head. At 18 px an
outlined shape with something solid inside it reads as a **darker** glyph, and
darkness survives where curvature does not.

Read this as the small-size rule pointed at families rather than at details:
the set's ceiling is about two members per silhouette that can be told apart by
shape, and the third has to differ in weight.
