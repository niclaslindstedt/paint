# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text lives at that link rather than being copied here, so it stays
current with upstream.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the
work. Harassment, personal attacks, and demeaning behaviour are not welcome in
issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour through the private channel documented in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) — it is the single place the project's contact
details are maintained, so they can't drift out of step between files. Reports
are handled confidentially, and the maintainers will respond as promptly as they
can.
