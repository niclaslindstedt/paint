The toolbar now opens with a bristle paintbrush, an airbrush that builds up where you linger, a paint bucket that fills an empty space up to the marks around it, and a dropper that draws with the colour you tap — plus a crayon, a calligraphy nib and a neon pen waiting in Settings → Tools.
