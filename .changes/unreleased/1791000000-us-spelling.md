title: US English
---

Paint's English is US English now, in its words as well as its spelling — "Color", "Watercolor", "gray", "Center", the "Pencil eraser" under "Erase", "Drawing paper" for cartridge, layers you check to merge, a trash can to delete — in the app and in its feature notes, and your drawings, presets and settings load exactly as before.
