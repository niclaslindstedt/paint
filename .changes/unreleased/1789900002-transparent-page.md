type: Changed
title: A page can be made of nothing
doc: canvas-theme
---

The page colour now opens on a chequer that means no page at all — the marks land on transparency, the canvas draws the chequer under them in the app's own greys, and a PNG or an SVG of that image downloads with nothing behind it without your finding a setting for it (JPG has no transparency, so it keeps the colour the sheet would have had).
