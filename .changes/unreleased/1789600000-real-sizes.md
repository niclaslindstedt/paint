---
type: Changed
title: Every tool comes in the sizes it is really made in
doc: plugins
---

A page pixel is now one dot of a 300 dpi print, so widths are millimetres of
paper: the pencil offers the four leads a mechanical pencil takes, the round
brush a #2 through a one-inch flat, type is set in points, and every tool's
slider spends its middle four tenths on the range that tool is genuinely made
in.
