New drawing is now **New image** — as often as not what starts there is a photo off the disk or a screenshot off the clipboard — it fills the screen on a phone with its title and its Create button pinned while the questions scroll, and it asks the page's **colour** and its **canvas type** beside its size, painting the six stocks on the colour you just picked and giving them a **Grain** slider that repaints the whole shelf as you drag it.
