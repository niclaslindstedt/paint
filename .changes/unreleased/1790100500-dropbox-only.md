---
type: Removed
title: Google Drive sync
---

Google Drive is no longer one of the places drawings can be kept. This device,
a folder you pick and Dropbox remain.

If you had Drive connected, the app falls back to this device. Your drawings
and their pictures stay in their folder in your own Drive, where you can read,
export or delete them as you would any other file — nothing is removed from
your account.
