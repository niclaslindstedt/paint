type: Added
title: A rubber, not just an eraser
doc: plugins
---

Switch the **rubber** on in Settings → Tools for an eraser that behaves like a real one: it lifts pencil and crayon a little at a time, leaves the paper's grain showing through, never quite takes all of it, and leaves ink, paint and marker exactly where they are — so you can sketch in pencil, ink over the sketch, and rub the sketch out.
