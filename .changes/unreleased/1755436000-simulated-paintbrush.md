title: Simulated paintbrush
doc: plugins
---

The round and flat brushes are one paintbrush now, and it simulates its paint
the way the pencil and calligraphy pen do: a finite dip spent over the page's
own grain, a flatness dial that runs the head from a round to a one-stroke
flat, and dry-brush scumble where the paint gives out.
